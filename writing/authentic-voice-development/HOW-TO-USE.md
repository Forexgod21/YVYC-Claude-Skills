## What This Skill Does

Finds a writer's voice by subtraction instead of affectation. Voice
treated as perception before style, the borrowed and performed
stripped away until the writer's honest register remains, the
sharp-friend calibration for what authentic sounds like, idiosyncrasy
protected as the fingerprint, consistency built across a body of work,
and an honest question that guards both the silence of the
credential-hedge and the mannerism of over-developed voice.

## When It Activates

- Developing a personal writing style
- "My writing sounds generic" / "sounds like someone else"
- Breaking out of stiff, hedged, or imitative prose
- Keeping voice consistent across a body of work
- Distinctive first-person writing

## Installation

1. Create a folder named `authentic-voice-development` in your Claude
   skills location.
2. Place `SKILL.md` inside it.
3. Claude activates it automatically on individual-voice work.
4. Distinct from `brand-voice-codex` (organizational voice, designed);
   pairs with `narrative-spine-architecture` (structure) and
   `prose-precision-protocol` (whose voice guard shares this
   discipline).

## Example Invocations

> "My writing sounds generic and I don't know why."

The subtraction diagnosis: generic is almost always voice sought by
addition and normalization. Claude strips the borrowed cadences,
performance vocabulary, and credential hedges, then finds the honest
perception underneath to build on.

> "How do I develop my writing voice?"

Perception before style: the work starts with what you actually
notice and think — captured in the sharp-friend register — because
style is downstream of perception, and affectation is just perception
borrowed from someone else.

> "I write like my favorite author and I want to sound like me."

Imitation reframed as a learning tool with an exit: study how the
effect is achieved, then translate it into your register instead of
wearing the author's skin. Keep only the cadences that have genuinely
become yours.

> "Do my pieces sound like the same writer?"

The body-of-work review: several pieces read together to find the
through-line — the recurring moves, the stance, the fingerprint —
then that through-line strengthened deliberately across future work.

## What You Get

- Voice found by removing the borrowed, not adding the affected
- Perception as the anchor, style as its downstream
- The sharp-friend register as a reliable authenticity check
- Idiosyncrasy protected instead of normalized away
- A recognizable through-line across a body of work
- A guard against both the hedge and the mannerism

## What This Skill Will Refuse

- Building voice by affectation and bigger words
- Normalizing idiosyncrasy toward safe and generic
- Wearing an admired author's skin as a destination
- Sanding voice away in the name of "professional"
- Performing "strong voice" until it curdles into mannerism
