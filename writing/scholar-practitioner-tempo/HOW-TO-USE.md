## What This Skill Does

Tempo is the base `scholar-practitioner` skill locked onto one specific
mission: Walden University MS in Industrial and Organizational Psychology
coursework, executed at PhD standard, on a 90-day sprint cadence. It
inherits the full doctrine — adversarial evaluator gate, theory-as-
mechanism, argument architecture, voice standard — and adds the
program-specific operating layer: APA 7 precision, Walden template
discipline, rubric-dominant execution, and I/O psychology theory
calibration.

It is also the reference example of how to build a **program-calibrated
edition** of the base skill. To adapt it to your own program, replace the
Program Context block (institution, degree, domain, theory canon) and the
template rules — the doctrine layer needs no changes.

## The Citation Guard

The most important rule in the skill: the pre-loaded theory canon
(Herzberg, Bandura, Schein, Kotter, and the rest) is a **selection
guide, not a reference list**. The skill will name a fitting theory,
then stop and ask for the real source before generating any citation.
It never fabricates a publication, journal, volume, page number, or
DOI. A fabricated citation in a graded submission is an academic
integrity violation — the skill flags and waits, every time.

## Deliverable Defaults

| Type | Default | Signature Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Discussion post | 200–300 words | Opens with a concrete organizational example, closes with judgment or Socratic question |
| Response post | 100–150 words | Engages one specific idea; never summarizes, never validates without analysis |
| Papers | Per rubric/template | Every heading maps to a rubric criterion; gate runs per section |
| Literature review | Per rubric | Synthesis by theme, never source-by-source annotation |

---

## Experience as Evidence

Military leadership, veteran advocacy, and consulting experience are
treated as organizational evidence, not personal anecdote — framed in
unit/mission/outcome/constraint terms and connected to the theoretical
mechanism in play. The skill balances them: never suppressed when they
are the strongest evidence, never over-relied on when the assignment
calls for broader context.

---

## Stand-Down Condition

Tempo holds PhD-level standard from first prompt to final submission —
no softening with session length, no drift with conversational tone —
until the operator explicitly ends the task or switches to a
non-academic mode.
