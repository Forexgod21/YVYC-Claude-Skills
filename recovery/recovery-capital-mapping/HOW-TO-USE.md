## What This Skill Does

Reorients recovery planning from the hole to the ground. Four capital
domains — personal, social, community, cultural — surveyed in the
person's own words, an honest ledger of assets AND exposures, plans
where every goal names the existing capital it builds from, identity
and role anchors surfaced as the deep assets they are, and a map the
person owns and re-walks as ground is gained.

## When It Activates

- Strengths-based recovery planning
- Recovery capital assessments
- Reintegration planning for veterans and returning citizens
- Relapse-risk context mapping
- Recovery community program design
- Any plan that currently starts from deficits

## Installation

1. Create a folder named `recovery-capital-mapping` in your Claude
   skills location.
2. Place `SKILL.md` inside it.
3. Claude activates it automatically on recovery planning work.
4. Pairs with `peer-support-session-frame` (the Work phase runs this
   map) and feeds `advocacy-campaign-doctrine` and
   `grant-narrative-architecture` (community gaps documented across
   maps become campaign evidence and needs statements).

## Example Invocations

> "Build a recovery plan template for our veteran peer program."

The four-domain survey built on stories over scores, the honest
ledger format, goal architecture where every objective names its
capital foundation — with military identity, role capital, and
battle-buddy social capital surfaced deliberately, because for
veterans those are load-bearing walls.

> "This person has so many barriers — where do we even start?"

The reorientation the doctrine exists for: survey the ground before
planning around the hole. First goals sequenced on the STRONGEST
capital, because early wins are themselves capital and momentum is
a domain asset.

> "Map relapse risk for someone returning to their old neighborhood."

The exposure column, scouted like terrain: specific relationships,
places, paydays, and stressors — named factually without moralizing —
alongside the asset column the plan will actually maneuver from.

> "Our resource list is outdated."

Community capital verification: which meetings run, which programs
have openings, which employers actually hire — plus the referral
intelligence loop so every person served makes the map better for
the next.

## What You Get

- Plans authored by the person, not prescribed to a patient
- An honest ledger — strengths-based without being deficit-blind
- Goals standing on verified ground
- Identity anchors doing the heavy lifting willpower alone drops
- A community map that compounds across everyone served
- Visible progress: today's map against the first map

## What This Skill Will Refuse

- Deficit-only assessments dressed as plans
- Positivity that ignores real exposure
- Services replacing capital that exists
- Unverified resource lists that spend hope on dead numbers
- Maps owned by the program instead of the person
