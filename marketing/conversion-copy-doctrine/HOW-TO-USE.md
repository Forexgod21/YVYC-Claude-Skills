## What This Skill Does

Fixes copy at the aim level, where it actually fails. One defined
reader at a chosen awareness level, one kept-able promise, every claim
carrying reachable proof, one action per piece, a skim path that
argues the whole case alone — and a test gate with a stranger test,
claim audit, and measurement hooks before anything ships. Honesty
constraints are structural: no manufactured scarcity, no fake urgency,
no invented social proof.

## When It Activates

- Sales pages, landing pages, product descriptions, store listings
- Email sequences, ads, calls to action
- "Make this more persuasive"
- Reviewing copy that is not converting
- Any writing where a signup, purchase, or click is the goal

## Installation

1. Create a folder named `conversion-copy-doctrine` in your Claude
   skills location.
2. Place `SKILL.md` inside it.
3. Claude activates it automatically on conversion copywriting work.
4. Pairs with `brand-voice-codex` (the voice the copy speaks in) and
   `organic-content-engine` (the audience the copy converts).

## Example Invocations

> "Write a product page for my store."

Aim first: who the one reader is, what problem in their words, what
awareness level, what single action — then the page: promise up top,
features translated to outcomes, proof beside every claim, objections
answered where they arise, one button.

> "My landing page gets traffic but no signups."

The aim-level audit: reader definition, awareness mismatch in the
opening, competing calls to action, claims without proof, and the
skim-path test — because most underperforming copy failed at
definition, not wording.

> "Make it more urgent."

Real urgency only: genuine deadlines, real inventory, actual cohort
starts. Manufactured scarcity and fake countdowns are refused
structurally — they convert once and poison being believed, the
brand's most valuable asset.

> "Review my sales email sequence."

Per-email single actions, promise consistency across the sequence,
claim audit against what the business can keep, read-aloud rhythm
pass, and the friction check at every point of action.

## What You Get

- Copy aimed at a defined reader instead of polished for nobody
- Promises the product keeps — optimized for the second purchase
- Claims a skeptic can check without leaving the page
- One clear action with its friction engineered down
- A skim path that converts the reader of headlines alone
- Measurement that turns every launch into learning

## What This Skill Will Refuse

- Drafting before reader, promise, and action are defined
- Overpromises that convert once and refund forever
- Manufactured scarcity, fake urgency, invented social proof
- Competing calls to action
- Launching without measurement hooks
