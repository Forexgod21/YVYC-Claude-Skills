## What This Skill Does

Replaces random acts of posting with an engine the creator can run
for years: three to five purpose-mapped pillars, a cadence set at
worst-week capacity, a repurposing ladder that turns one source piece
into many native expressions, honest hooks written last, measurement
that tracks the audience asset instead of applause, and a
reduced-capacity mode designed before it is needed — because organic
content compounds only for creators still publishing when the
compounding arrives.

## When It Activates

- Content strategy and posting cadence design
- Content pillars, repurposing systems, platform selection
- Audience growth without ad spend
- "I post constantly and nothing happens"
- Creator burnout and sustainability planning

## Installation

1. Create a folder named `organic-content-engine` in your Claude
   skills location.
2. Place `SKILL.md` inside it.
3. Claude activates it automatically on content marketing and social
   strategy work.
4. Pairs with `brand-voice-codex` (the voice the engine publishes in)
   and `conversion-copy-doctrine` (where the audience the engine
   builds gets converted).

## Example Invocations

> "Build me a content strategy."

Pillars first: the three to five themes mapped to audience needs and
offer lanes, each with its purpose — then the worst-week cadence,
per-platform and honest, the batching structure, and the asset
scoreboard. The calendar comes last, because a calendar without an
engine is a burnout schedule.

> "I've been posting daily for two months and I'm exhausted."

The survivable-cadence rebuild: cadence reset to worst-week capacity,
batching installed, the repurposing ladder doing the volume work, and
the reduced-capacity mode designed now — because consistency at three
a week for two years beats daily for six weeks then silence.

> "How do I get more out of my long-form content?"

The ladder: one source piece translated — not copy-pasted — into
platform-native clips, quotes, graphics, and short-form variants,
with the overperformers running the ladder backward into full-length
treatments the audience already voted for.

> "My posts get likes but my business gets nothing."

The scoreboard swap: vanity metrics demoted to weather, asset metrics
promoted — owned-audience growth, saves, conversations, traffic to
owned property — plus the rented-land rule converting borrowed reach
into channels that survive any algorithm change.

## What You Get

- An engine, not a sprint: pillars, cadence, ladder, scoreboard
- Volume without daily creation pressure
- Hooks that earn attention honestly and keep it
- Growth measured in the asset being built
- A system that survives the creator's hard weeks
- An audience that increasingly lives on land the brand owns

## What This Skill Will Refuse

- Calendars before pillars
- Cadences set at inspired-week capacity
- Copy-paste cross-posting as distribution
- Bait-and-switch hooks
- Vanity-metric scoreboards
- Engines that require the creator to break themselves
