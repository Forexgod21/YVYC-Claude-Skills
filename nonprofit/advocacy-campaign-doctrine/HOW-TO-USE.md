## What This Skill Does

Turns "raising awareness" into campaigns that move decisions. Every
campaign gets a decision target (the change, the decider with actual
authority, the yes-able ask), a power map that sends energy to
persuadables, a story-data interlock with absolute consent doctrine,
an escalation ladder climbed rung by earned rung, and a win ledger
that counts position and capacity even when the decision is lost.

## When It Activates

- Policy change and awareness campaigns
- Community organizing and coalition building
- Public comment drives, testimony preparation, letter campaigns
- "Get the county to..." / "build support for..." / "change the
  policy"
- Any effort to move an institution or a decision-maker

## Installation

1. Create a folder named `advocacy-campaign-doctrine` in your Claude
   skills location.
2. Place `SKILL.md` inside it.
3. Claude activates it automatically on advocacy and organizing work.
4. Pairs with `program-logic-model` (the evidence of what works) and
   `grant-narrative-architecture` (funding the capacity the campaign
   builds).

## Example Invocations

> "We want to raise awareness about veteran mental health in our
> county."

The doctrine converts it: awareness becomes ammunition for a decision
target — which body, which budget cycle, which yes-able ask — or the
campaign gets named as the values statement it currently is, and
rebuilt.

> "Help me prepare testimony for the county commission."

Story-data interlock in testimony sequence: the consented human story
that opens the door, the local numbers that hold it open, the specific
ask that walks through — sized to the time limit, aimed at the
persuadable commissioners the power map identified.

> "Our coalition keeps arguing about tactics."

The diagnosis the doctrine predicts: strategic agreement was assumed,
never written. The fix: shared goal signed, roles explicit — who
speaks, who convenes, who holds which relationships — and the
engagement ladder built inside the coalition itself.

> "We won! The policy passed."

The campaign's final phase begins: monitoring the yes until it is
implemented, because a policy adopted is not a policy real — plus the
mandatory debrief while memory is fresh.

## What You Get

- Campaigns defined by decisions, not sentiments
- A power map that stops preaching to the choir
- Messengers the decider cannot dismiss
- Story governance that protects the people the cause serves
- Escalation that preserves tomorrow's relationships
- Wins that get enforced, and losses that build the rematch

## What This Skill Will Refuse

- Campaigns with no decider or no yes-able ask
- Spending energy converting the committed opposition
- Using anyone's story without written, withdrawable consent
- Skipping the ladder to scorched earth
- Ending the campaign at the spoken yes
