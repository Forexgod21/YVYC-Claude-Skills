## What This Skill Does

Forces the program's claim onto paper where it can be tested, funded,
and defended. Five links from inputs to impact, an if-then-because
test on every arrow, assumptions and external factors on the page,
measurement hooks installed at design time, dosage honesty, and a
living-model rule so the diagram never drifts into funder fiction.

## When It Activates

- Designing any nonprofit or community program
- Funder requests for a logic model or theory of change
- Impact measurement and evaluation planning
- "How do we show this works"
- Any program running on conviction instead of a stated causal chain

## Installation

1. Create a folder named `program-logic-model` in your Claude skills
   location.
2. Place `SKILL.md` inside it.
3. Claude activates it automatically on program design and evaluation
   work.
4. Pairs with `grant-narrative-architecture` — the logic model IS the
   golden thread that skill audits, drawn as a system.

## Example Invocations

> "Build a logic model for our veteran peer support program."

Five links populated left to right, every arrow read aloud with its
mechanism, evidence lineage cited where prior research supports an
arrow, assumptions and external factors on the model, and every
outcome carrying its measure, source, and cadence before the first
participant arrives.

> "The funder wants a theory of change."

Same chain, deepened at the arrows: the mechanisms, the evidence
behind them, the load-bearing assumptions flagged — delivered at
three altitudes: one-page visual for the funder, detailed version
for staff, plain-language version for the community.

> "How do we prove our program works?"

Measurement hooks audit: what is counted, by whom, how often, against
what baseline — plus the honesty rules that survive an evaluator:
dose stated, starters and completers both reported, external factors
named so attribution claims hold.

## What You Get

- A causal chain someone can challenge — which is what makes it
  credible
- Arrows with mechanisms, not hopes
- Measurement designed in, not scrambled at reporting time
- Attribution claims that survive contact with an evaluator
- One model, three translations: funder, staff, community

## What This Skill Will Refuse

- Arrows without mechanisms, decorated over instead of marked
- Models with no assumptions or external factors row
- Outcomes with no measure attached
- Completer-only reporting
- A frozen diagram describing an evolved program
