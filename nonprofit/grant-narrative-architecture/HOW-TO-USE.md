## What This Skill Does

Builds grant proposals on the funder's scoring skeleton instead of the
writer's passion. Funder intelligence gets gathered before drafting, the
needs statement stands on evidence-proximity-gap, one golden thread runs
from need to budget and gets audited in both directions, outcomes stay
measurable and honest, and a compliance matrix kills the technicality
disqualification.

## When It Activates

- Writing or reviewing any grant proposal or funding application
- Letters of inquiry, needs statements, RFP responses
- Budget narratives and sustainability sections
- "Write a grant" / "review my proposal"
- Deciding whether a funding opportunity is worth pursuing at all

## Installation

1. Create a folder named `grant-narrative-architecture` in your
   Claude skills location.
2. Place `SKILL.md` inside it.
3. Claude activates it automatically on grant and funding work.

## Example Invocations

> "Write a grant proposal for our veteran peer support program."

Funder intelligence first: priorities in their language, scoring
logic, portfolio behavior, honest fit verdict. Then the needs
statement — the community's need, quantified locally, with your
proximity claimed as the asset it is — and the golden thread from
need to budget with no breaks.

> "Review my draft before I submit."

Both-direction thread audit, outputs/outcomes separation check,
budget-narrative reconciliation, compliance matrix against the RFP,
and the tired-reviewer skim test: headings, first sentences, and
budget totals carrying the case alone.

> "Should we apply for this RFP?"

The fit verdict, delivered honestly — including "no" when the
portfolio scan says this funder does not fund your work, because a
mismatch pursued costs the hours and the relationship.

## What You Get

- Proposals built to score, not merely to describe
- A needs statement that belongs to the community
- A golden thread reviewers cannot break
- Outcomes the year-end report can actually show
- A budget that tells the same story in numbers
- Zero technicality disqualifications

## What This Skill Will Refuse

- Drafting before the funder intelligence file exists
- Needs statements about organizational hunger
- Conflating outputs with outcomes
- Budget lines with no narrative anchor
- Overpromised outcomes that become reporting liabilities
