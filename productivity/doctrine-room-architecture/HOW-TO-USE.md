## What This Skill Does

Builds the meta-layer that keeps multi-project operations coherent:
one doctrine room that ships no products and instead produces the
rules, standards, and decisions every other space runs on. Two-layer
separation, a rule lifecycle from observation to review, a decision
ledger that kills the re-litigation tax, and doctrine written to be
used under load — named, concise, enforceable.

## When It Activates

- Multi-project operations losing consistency
- "I keep re-deciding the same things"
- Designing workspace/project structures for complex operations
- Standing up operating doctrine for a team or solo operation
- Any standards question answered differently in different places

## Installation

1. Create a folder named `doctrine-room-architecture` in your Claude
   skills location.
2. Place `SKILL.md` inside it.
3. Claude activates it automatically on governance-layer and
   operating-structure work.
4. Pairs with `agent-stack-org-design` (the org chart the room
   governs) and `authority-stack-doctrine` (the instruction hierarchy
   inside each room).

## Example Invocations

> "My twelve projects all format, name, and decide things differently."

The two-layer separation, installed: a doctrine room stood up, the
scattered per-project rules harvested, unified through the adoption
step, and propagated back — with the propagation tracked, because an
unadopted rule governs nothing.

> "Set up a decision ledger for my operation."

The room's most valuable artifact: decision, date, reasoning,
alternatives declined, and a reversibility weight — standing law,
default-until-challenged, or experiment-with-review-date — so
revisiting becomes deliberate instead of drift.

> "Someone asked how we do things and I realized it's all in my head."

The transmission test, failed and fixed: single-home canon, current
version, and a handoff standard that makes a newcomer — human or AI —
functional from the documents alone.

> "This rule keeps fighting reality."

The lifecycle's fifth step: review triggers fire, the rule returns to
the room, and it gets revised or retired on the record — doctrine
that adapts on purpose instead of ossifying or eroding.

## What You Get

- One place where "how we work" gets written, versioned, and owned
- A re-litigation tax refund: decisions with dates and reasoning
- Projects that hold their shape as the operation scales
- Rules that get followed because they are named, concise, and
  enforceable
- An operation whose "how" survives transmission

## What This Skill Will Refuse

- Doctrine scattered across chats, docs, and memory
- Rules without visible failure modes
- The doctrine room absorbing tactical work
- Unpropagated canon counted as governance
- An operation that only works while its founder is in the room
