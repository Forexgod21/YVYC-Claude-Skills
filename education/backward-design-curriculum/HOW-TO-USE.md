## What This Skill Does

Enforces a fixed design order on anything meant to teach: name the
outcome first, define the evidence that would prove it, and only then
build the activity. Adds the discipline layers that make instruction
land: observable outcomes, alignment audits, cognitive load budgets,
scaffolds that fade, misconception-first design, and mastery gates on
compounding prerequisites.

## The Problem It Solves

Most teaching material is designed forward: interesting content, then
activities, then a test invented at the end. Learners attend
everything and can do nothing new afterward. Coverage gets mistaken
for learning, engagement gets mistaken for outcomes, and supports
never fade, so learners stay dependent on them.

## When It Activates

- Designing or reviewing any curriculum, course, unit, lesson,
  workshop, training, tutorial, or onboarding sequence
- Writing learning objectives, lesson plans, or assessments
- Converting existing content into teachable form for a stated
  audience, at any age level
- Any time an activity is proposed before its outcome has been named

## Installation

1. Create a folder named `backward-design-curriculum` in your Claude
   skills location.
2. Place `SKILL.md` inside it.
3. It fires on the design of instruction; pair it with the writing
   category skills when the vehicle is prose or story.

## Example Invocations

> "Build me a six-week course on budgeting."

Stage one first: what a graduate of week six can *do*, stated
observably. Then the evidence per week, then the sessions, with the
alignment audit run before delivery.

> "Make this fun for kids."

Fun as fuel, not destination: the vehicle changes to story, play, and
repetition; the outcome and the respect for the learner stay intact.
Every game traces to an outcome or it is cut.

> "Here's my lesson plan, thoughts?"

The audit, run backward: which outcome does each activity serve, what
evidence gates progress, where is the fade plan on each scaffold, and
which misconception does the design defeat. Activities tracing to
nothing get named.

> "Why do my students pass the quiz and fail the project?"

Assessment mismatch, diagnosed: the quiz measured recognition while
the project demanded production, and the design taught to the former.

## What Changes In Claude's Behavior

| Without This Skill | With This Skill |
|---|---|
| Content and activities first, assessment invented last | Outcome, then evidence, then activity, in that order |
| "Understand" and "appreciate" as objectives | Observable verbs two witnesses could agree on |
| Engaging activities justified by engagement | Every activity traces to an outcome or is cut |
| Five new concepts per segment | A load budget defensible for the stated audience |
| Templates and hints that never withdraw | Every scaffold ships with its fade plan |
| The right model taught over an intact wrong one | The misconception surfaced and visibly defeated first |
| "We covered it" as success | Demonstrated evidence as the only gate |

## What This Skill Will Refuse

- Shipping teaching material whose outcomes were written after the
  activities, as justification
- Outcome statements no observer could verify
- Advancing a design past an unmastered compounding prerequisite
- Dumbing content down past its own integrity in the name of age
  level
- Cutting respect for the learner as a shortcut to accessibility
- Treating a stalled learner as the defect instead of examining the
  instruction that stalled them
