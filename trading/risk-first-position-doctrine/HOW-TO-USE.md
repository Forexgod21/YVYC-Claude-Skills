## What This Skill Does

Enforces the order that keeps accounts alive: risk budget first,
invalidation second, size as arithmetic, entry last. Conviction never
sets size, stops live at structure and move one direction, correlated
positions count as one risk, and the drawdown protocol exists in
writing before the drawdown arrives.

## When It Activates

- "How much should I risk" / "size this position" / "where's my stop"
- Leverage decisions and account protection
- Drawdown handling and loss-limit design
- Any trade plan review, in any market
- BEFORE entry logic gets discussed — always

## Installation

1. Create a folder named `risk-first-position-doctrine` in your
   Claude skills location.
2. Place `SKILL.md` inside it.
3. Claude activates it automatically when position risk is in play.
4. Pairs with `chart-pattern-discipline` (supplies the structural
   invalidation level Step 2 requires) and `trade-journal-protocol`
   (supplies the records the drawdown review runs on).

## Example Invocations

> "I want to put half my account on this setup — it's perfect."

The doctrine, applied: size is budget ÷ stop distance, and "perfect"
is not a variable in that equation. You get the computed size, the
gap between it and half the account, and the reminder that "certain"
is a feeling while the ratio is a number.

> "Where should my stop go?"

At the invalidation level from the chart read — where the thesis is
wrong — not at a round number or pain tolerance. And if honoring
that structural stop within your risk budget makes the position too
small to bother with, the answer is no trade, not a closer fake stop.

> "I'm down 15% this month."

Drawdown protocol: size reduction per your written thresholds,
trading halt if the threshold demands it, and a journal-based review
answering the only question that matters — variance within a working
system, or a broken edge. "Win it back" does not get the floor.

## What You Get

- Position sizes computed by arithmetic, immune to conviction
- Stops that live at structure, in the market, moving one direction
- Correlation counted before it counts against you
- Loss limits enforced by procedure instead of willpower
- A drawdown protocol written in calm for use under fire
- Revenge trades flagged even when they win

## What This Skill Will Refuse

- Sizing by conviction, feel, or streak
- Mental stops
- Widening a stop mid-trade
- Treating three correlated tickets as three separate risks
- Letting "win it back" run the drawdown response
